DJ & Music

StemMaker

Turns your MP3, WAV and FLAC files into Traktor stem files (.stem.mp4) with drums, bass, melody and vocals. No Python, no setup. Since version 2.0 the Turbo engine is 6 to 10 times faster on the CPU and uses your graphics card when it helps. Includes StemPlayer to preview the stems. Installer or portable ZIP.

Network Ping Tool

Excel VBA tool for quickly pinging devices in industrial networks, e.g. Siemens CPUs, HMIs and PLCs over Profinet. Three scan modes, clean cancel, correct IP sorting.

Electronics

LedPreResistor

Calculates the series resistor for one or more LEDs: standard value from E12, E24 or E96, colour bands, power and recommended resistor size, with an estimate of how warm it gets. A step-by-step explanation shows the calculation with your own numbers, and a page about LEDs is built in. Single portable EXE (German interface for now).

LEDs · E12 / E24 / E96 · Windows
