DJ & Music

StemMaker

Turns your MP3, WAV and FLAC files into Traktor stem files (.stem.mp4) with drums, bass, melody and vocals. No Python, no setup. Since version 2.0 the Turbo engine is 6 to 10 times faster on the CPU and uses your graphics card when it helps. Includes StemPlayer to preview the stems and, new in 2.1, StemTagger for title, artist and cover. Installer or portable ZIP.

Traktor Pro 4 · Mixxx · Windows
To StemMaker

StemTagger

Sets title, artist, album, genre and cover art in Traktor stem files (.stem.mp4) and MP3s, for a whole folder at once. Only the tags are rewritten: the stems, the audio and Traktor's cue points, loops and beatgrid stay untouched. A column shows which MP3s Traktor has already analysed. Portable ZIP, also included in StemMaker as an add-on.

Electronics

LedPreResistor

Calculates the series resistor for one or more LEDs: standard value from E12, E24 or E96, colour bands, power and recommended resistor size, with an estimate of how warm it gets. A step-by-step explanation shows the calculation with your own numbers, and a page about LEDs is built in. Single portable EXE (German interface for now).
